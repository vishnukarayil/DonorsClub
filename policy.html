Donors' Club policy

Policy version 1.0, reviewed 24 October 2026.

1. Blood donation here is voluntary

Everyone who gives blood through this blood centre does so freely, without payment of any kind. The club exists to thank and recognise voluntary donors and to remind them when they are next able to donate. It is not a way to buy, sell or earn blood.

2. What the club offers

Membership offers recognition and care, never money or anything with a money value:

  • a digital donor card and a profile showing your tier;
  • thank-you messages and a reminder of when you can next donate;
  • certificates, pins, badges, medals and keepsakes of little or no intrinsic value;
  • a free basic health check (blood pressure, weight, haemoglobin) when you donate;
  • refreshments at the centre;
  • your name on the Wall of Donors, and invitations to donor appreciation events;
  • convenience, such as help with booking a donation slot.

3. What the club does not do

  • No cash, cheques, vouchers, coupons or points that can be exchanged for money.
  • No discounts, concessions or free services of monetary value, for example on laboratory tests or hospital bills.
  • No blood is promised, reserved or priced for a donor or the donor's family because of their tier.
  • Nobody is asked to donate in order to qualify for something, and nobody is pressured to donate more often than is safe for them.

4. Tiers

Tiers (Silver and upwards) simply count how many times you have donated, as a way of saying thank you. They have no effect on whether you are fit to donate. That is decided on the day by a medical examination, and the medical officer's decision always comes first. Tier names and the recognition items attached to them can change, as set out below.

5. Safety comes first

You may donate only if you are declared fit after a medical examination and the minimum interval since your last donation has passed. Please answer the health questions honestly. Your safety and the safety of the patient who receives your blood matter more than any badge. Your blood is tested for the mandatory infection markers (HIV, hepatitis B and C, syphilis and malaria), and you can ask the blood centre for your results.

6. Changes to this policy

Tier levels and membership benefits are subject to change as per our policies. We may add, change or withdraw items at any time, in particular to stay in line with the law and national guidelines. Benefits are valid for a limited period after each donation, shown on your profile.

7. Your information

We store the details you give (name, date of birth, gender, blood group, place, mobile number, whether you work at Ahalia, and your donation dates) only to run the club and to contact you about donations. Cards you receive carry a code that works as your password: keep it private. Anyone who scans a card's QR code sees only a first name and last initial, the tier and the dates.

8. The rules this policy follows

This policy is written to follow these provisions:

  • Drugs and Cosmetics Rules, 1940, Rule 122EA. A donor is a person who donates blood voluntarily, after being declared fit, without accepting in return any consideration in cash or in kind. Pins, plaques, badges, medals, commendation certificates, time off from work, membership of a blood assurance programme and gifts of little or intrinsic monetary value are not treated as consideration. A professional or paid donor, who gives blood for valuable consideration, is not a donor under the Rules.
  • Ministry of Health and Family Welfare, Guidelines on voluntary blood donation (2025). A voluntary non-remunerated donor receives no payment in cash or in kind that could substitute for money. Small tokens, refreshments and reimbursement of direct travel costs are compatible with this. Incentives should not influence the decision to donate, should not depend on donating, and should not have a monetary value. Acceptable examples include health screening, promotional items, recognition items such as certificates, pins and badges, free refreshments and time off work. The guidelines also encourage thank-you messages and next-due-date reminders to keep donors donating.
